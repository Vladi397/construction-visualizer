  1. Surface Manual

    Four corners plus an add and remove brush. The trained segmentation model replaces this step at M4.

  2. Plane Working

    A homography from the four corners. The missing side and the area in m² come from perspective.

  3. Material Working

    Seeded sampling from a sheet cut out of a finished job. Scale is locked to centimetres, only the offset changes.

  4. Light Working

    A median-filtered light map of the original surface is multiplied over the new material, so shadows stay put.
