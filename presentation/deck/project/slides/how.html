HOW IT WORKS

Photo in. Real stone out.

1 Photo

2 Flat paste

3 Perspective

4 Light and shadow

Four steps. Mark the surface, then a plain paste looks like a sticker. Perspective makes the stones get smaller with distance. Finally the photo's own light and shadow go back on top, so the house shadow stays where it was. Every stone comes from a photo of a real finished job, no generative AI.
